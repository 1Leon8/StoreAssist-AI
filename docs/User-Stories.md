# **StoreAssist AI**

## **User Stories**

### **1. Customer Communication**

As a retail associate,  
I want to capture a customer's speech,  
so that I can understand what they are asking for.

---

### **2. Language Translation**

As a retail associate,  
I want the customer's request translated into a language I understand,  
so that I can communicate with customers who speak another language.

---

### **3. Product Understanding**

As a retail associate,  
I want the system to identify important product information from a customer's request,  
so that I can find the correct product.

---

### **4. Product Search**

As a retail associate,  
I want to search for products using natural language,  
so that I do not need to know the exact product name.

---

### **5. Product Variations**

As a retail associate,  
I want to see different sizes and variations of a product,  
so that I can help the customer find the appropriate option.

---

### **6. Inventory**

As a retail associate,  
I want to see product inventory,  
so that I know whether the product is available.

---

### **7. Product Location**

As a retail associate,  
I want to see the aisle and bay for a product,  
so that I can quickly locate it in the store.

---

### **8. Combined Workflow**

As a retail associate,  
I want translation, product search, inventory, and product location in one application,  
so that I do not need to switch between multiple systems.
